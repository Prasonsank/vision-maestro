Common Eye Care FAQs

What is included in a 21-step eye test?

It includes auto-refraction, muscle balance testing, slit-lamp biomicroscopy, intraocular pressure measurement, and a detailed retina screening.

How long does it take to get my new glasses?

Most single-vision glasses are ready within 24 hours. Complex lenses may take 3-5 working days for precision surfacing.
